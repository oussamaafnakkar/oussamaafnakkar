## 🧭 About me

I'm a backend developer who graduated from the **ALX Software Engineering** program. I write Python, Flask, Django and Node.js, and I'm drawn to the parts of computing that are quiet, low-level and security-minded: operating systems, protocols, and the networks that carry our calls and payments.

I run a technical blog about **telecom and payment security**, where I try to explain how these systems work and where they can break.

## ✍️ My blog

```
oussama@sbytec:~$ ls sbytec.com/
blog/  vulnerabilities/  access-denied/  rabbit-hole/
```

**[sbytec.com](https://www.sbytec.com/)** is my independent security publication, written from the intersection of **telecom infrastructure and security**. It has four sections:

| | Section | What you'll find |
|---|---|---|
| 📓 | **Blog** | Field notes, technical breakdowns and research dispatches. Independently verified, never padded for length. |
| 🛰️ | **Vulnerabilities** | Structured analyses of systemic flaws in telecom protocols, mobile network infrastructure and signaling architecture. Original analysis for practitioners, not a CVE dump. |
| 🔐 | **Access Denied** | Deep dives into authentication failures, unauthorized access vectors, privilege escalation chains and the design decisions that make them possible. |
| 🐇 | **Rabbit Hole** | Long-form research that follows a single technical question to its conclusion: surveillance infrastructure, algorithmic systems, trust-model failures and the hidden architecture of modern technology. |

[**Read the blog →**](https://www.sbytec.com/)
